Research

Master's Thesis Researcher · Dal Peraro Lab, EPFL Aug 2025 – Feb 2026

Going from a chemical reaction to a plausible enzyme for it. A graph variational autoencoder learns embeddings of reactions (92% accuracy on top-level enzyme classification, 0.87 AUROC overall), and a language model generates candidate sequences from those embeddings. The proteins it produced had no match in BLAST and scored comparably to wild-type on predicted activity. Nominated for the EPFL thesis award.
